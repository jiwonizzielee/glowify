The Glowify App

Your skin health starts here

A free app for teens that explains how the sun, your skin, and everyday habits fit together. No account, health details, or photos needed.

Open the App
What's Inside

Learn, check, and build habits

Learn

Short, research-backed modules on sun safety and skin cancer prevention. Four modules take about 20 minutes in total, with quizzes to check what you know.

Check the UV

A live hourly UV forecast for your location, with suggested protection for each level.

Build habits

Daily challenges, myth cards, and a learning streak with points and badges to keep you going.

Ready to try it?

Glowify shares research-backed information but does not diagnose or treat conditions, and does not replace a visit with a healthcare professional.
