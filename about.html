About Glowify

Awareness and community, for healthier skin.

Glowify is an organization committed to supporting individuals with skin-related concerns and raising awareness about the importance of skincare, skin cancer prevention, and overall skin health.

Our Story

Why we do this work

Skin health is often overlooked — until it isn't. Skin cancer is one of the most common and most preventable cancers, yet awareness and access to basic skincare education and supplies remain uneven. Glowify exists to close that gap.

We bring skin health education into classrooms, we show up for patients undergoing treatment with handwritten encouragement, and we put practical skincare essentials directly into the hands of people who need them. Awareness, compassion, and access — that's the work, and it takes a community to do it well.

What We Stand For

Our values

Awareness

Education is prevention — we make skin health knowledge clear, accessible, and easy to act on.

Compassion

Behind every letter and every kit is a person — we lead with empathy in everything we make.

Community

Real change happens together — through volunteers, schools, patients, and neighbors alike.

Prevention

Sun safety and early detection save lives — we champion practical habits people can keep.

How We Work

Three programs, one mission

1

Educating Youth on Skin Health

Interactive school presentations that cover the fundamentals of skin health, the early signs of skin cancer, and practical prevention strategies students can carry with them.

2

Supporting Skin Cancer Patients

Letter-writing events where participants craft personalized, encouraging letters that uplift and inspire patients throughout treatment.

3

Providing Skincare Packages

Skincare kits delivered to individuals across New York — sunscreen, moisturizer, lip balm, soap, and a simple guide for caring for skin in different weather conditions.
