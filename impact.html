Our Programs

Where the impact happens

School Presentations

Interactive sessions covering skin health fundamentals, early warning signs of skin cancer, and prevention strategies students can use right away.

Letter-Writing Events

Community gatherings where participants write personalized, encouraging letters for individuals undergoing skin cancer treatment.

Skincare Kits

Kits packed with sunscreen, moisturizer, lip balm, soap, and a weather-based skincare guide, delivered to individuals across New York.
