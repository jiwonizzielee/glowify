Our Mission

A supportive movement that turns awareness into action

At Glowify, we believe in the power of awareness and community to create a healthier future for our skin. Join our movement of advocates dedicated to promoting skin cancer prevention, spreading sun safety education, and empowering others to make informed choices. We believe protecting your skin should feel as natural as caring for the rest of your health.

What We Do

Three ways we show up for skin health

Youth Education

Educating Youth on Skin Health

We provide skincare education through interactive school presentations designed to engage students directly — covering the fundamentals of skin health, the early signs of skin cancer, and practical prevention strategies.

Patient Support

Supporting Skin Cancer Patients

We organize letter-writing events to support individuals undergoing treatment for skin cancer, where participants craft personalized, encouraging letters that uplift and inspire patients throughout their journey.

Skincare Kits

Providing Skincare Packages

We provide skincare kits to individuals in New York, each including sunscreen, moisturizer, lip balm, and soap — plus a simple guide for caring for skin in different weather conditions.
